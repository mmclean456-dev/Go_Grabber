# Quest of the Dragon's Gold

A cross-genre point-and-click adventure game featuring knights, pirates, cowboys, and medieval fantasy elements.

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser
2. Click "Begin Your Quest" to start
3. Click anywhere on the map to move your knight
4. Click on NPCs to interact with them

## Controls

- **Left Click**: Move to location / Interact with NPC
- **Right Click**: Cancel / Close dialogue
- **I**: Open/Close Inventory
- **Q**: Open/Close Quest Log
- **ESC**: Close all panels
- **1-4**: Use hotbar items

## Game Features

### Core Gameplay
- Point-and-click movement in a large scrolling 2D world
- Turn-based combat system with various actions
- Equipment and inventory management
- Gold economy with shops and upgrades

### Regions to Explore
- **Starting Village**: Begin your journey, talk to the Elder
- **Forest**: Fight wild beasts, recruit the wizard Merlin
- **Pirate Cove**: Challenge Captain Blackbeard to cards for a ship
- **Western Town**: Help the Sheriff catch outlaws, gamble in saloons
- **Medieval Castle**: Meet the Ghost King, recruit Sir Roderick
- **Mystic Swamp**: Trade with the Swamp Witch
- **Mountain Pass**: Find the final path to the dragon
- **Dragon's Lair**: Face the final boss!

### The Main Quest
Gather clues from across the land to find the Dragon's Lair:
1. Talk to the Blacksmith about the dragon's weakness
2. Beat Captain Blackbeard at cards for his map knowledge
3. Help the Sheriff and learn from the townspeople
4. Speak with the Ghost King in the castle
5. Trade with the Swamp Witch for final secrets

### Side Activities
- **Gambling**: Play poker in taverns and saloons
- **Bounty Hunting**: Capture outlaws for the Sheriff
- **Ally Recruitment**: Build a party with knights and wizards
- **Gear Upgrades**: Buy better weapons and armor

## Tips

- Save gold for better equipment before facing tougher enemies
- Gather all clues before attempting the Dragon's Lair
- Recruit allies to help in combat
- Use health potions in tough battles
- The dragon is level 20 - prepare well!

## Technical Details

Built with vanilla HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. No external dependencies required.

- Procedurally generated terrain
- Dynamic NPC dialogue system
- Turn-based combat with stats
- Persistent quest and inventory state
